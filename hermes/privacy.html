Privacy policy

This policy applies to the Hermes personal Google integration operated by Rohan Bansal for personal productivity. It does not describe every other deployment of Hermes Agent.

Google data accessed and why

With the owner's authorization, this integration can access Gmail message content, headers, attachments and labels; calendar events and calendar metadata; Drive files and metadata; Docs and Sheets content; and contacts. It uses this access to fulfill requested email, scheduling, document and contact tasks, including specifically configured automations. Write operations can include sending messages or changing calendars and documents when authorized.

Processing and sharing

Relevant task content may be processed by the configured AI model provider and by enabled tools or services needed to fulfill the task. Responses may be delivered through the interface the owner uses, including enabled messaging services such as Discord. The owner should use an appropriate private destination for sensitive material. When the owner requests sending an email, sharing a document or another external action, the relevant data is sent to the intended service or recipient.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google data is accessed for the personal-assistant functionality described here, not for an unrelated commercial data product. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. External providers handle received content under their applicable terms and account settings.

Storage, retention and safeguards

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's system and are not published on this website. Retrieved content and task outputs may remain in local assistant conversations, downloaded files, logs, backups, or the services used to process and deliver the task. There is no single automatic deletion period for all of these locations. Retention depends on the relevant local files, service settings and backups. Access to Google APIs uses HTTPS; access to the operator's system and credentials should be restricted to authorized users.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously downloaded content, conversation history, backups or copies held by recipients. The owner can delete those copies in the systems where they are stored, subject to each provider's deletion and backup policies. For help with access or deletion, contact rohan.bansal@gmail.com.

Public website

These informational pages contain no application sign-in, analytics scripts, or data-entry forms. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary web-request metadata under its own privacy policy. No Google account content is published as part of these pages.

Changes

Material changes to this integration's data practices will be reflected here. This policy was updated October 27, 2026.
